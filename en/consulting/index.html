How We Can Work Together

I work with organizations, managers, and leaders in tech, media, education, and beyond. I start by listening and learning what works and what doesn't; if needed, I run an organizational diagnosis. Then I ask questions, sort it all out, and hand it back to you folded, clean, and pressed – and from there the solution usually shows up on its own.

OKRs and Periodic Work Plans

Pretty goals nobody ever looks at – everyone's got those. I offer a collaborative process where goals are fed from every layer of the organization: the kind people come back to, not because a report is due soon, but because they actually help make decisions.

Follow-Up Is Painful

We all know it – there's a meeting, sometimes even a long process, a decision gets made, there's a sense of accomplishment, and then… nothing happens. Or worse – something happens, just not what we decided.

I came to this service after someone tried to recruit me as a Chief of Staff. I saw the pain was real, but the fix is in how things are run – and whoever gets hired will fall into the same machinery unless we first understand, together and in depth, what isn't working and solve it (spoiler: it's probably about you). We figure out together what's broken in the way things get implemented – or don't – after a decision, and build something different. Usually that doesn't involve hiring, and if it does – after the process it's far easier to find the right person and set them up to succeed.

If an ongoing need for operational coordination comes up, I bring the right person in as part of the service.

Time-Management Workshops That Actually Stick

Most workshops teach principles. People walk out full of inspiration, maybe do things differently for a day, two, a week – and then… everything slides back to the old habits. With me it works differently.

We start by understanding your organization: which AI tools you already have, where they're going unused, what the team's real pain is. Then we meet, work through the basic principles together, and build a method that connects those principles to the tools you've already paid for. And we measure. Not a feeling of improvement – we actually measure.

Your investment in AI tools should show up as team effectiveness. This is where that happens.

Managing Processes and Change

A change in org structure. A work plan that needs to land in reality. Even an office move. These are the projects that look managerial on paper but really demand two things that rarely come together: strategic problem-solving and a sharp sense for internal communication. I bring both – from the stage where you break the problem down to the moment people understand why.

Manager Coaching (1:1) for Clarity

Support and counsel for managers, with a focus on moments of change, stepping into a role, or coping with overload – breaking through blocks and building a practical work plan. Together, honestly and with courage, we identify where you are in your professional development and build a path from there.

A place to create clarity, get someone in your corner, and work out what you need to move forward. Especially good in moments of change: stepping into a senior role, leading a complex project, or feeling worn out and wondering about what's next. The managers who come to me are brave ones who want to grow, ready to take an honest look at what needs to be done differently, take responsibility, and change. We meet regularly, break down what's stuck, and build a plan you can actually work with – on both the personal and the organizational level.

Breakthroughs From the Past Two Years

  • Building a PIP for an employee who genuinely improved their performance and found their place again under the same manager.
  • Building a lean OKR plan (I swear) that fit the organization's character, and seeing it through.

  • Reorganizing an R&D arm, including redefining roles and processes from scratch (because AI, of course).
