Bringing clarity to complex processes.

I help managers and organizations turn a mess into a work plan they're actually excited to start. Together we pin down the goals worth chasing, build the work processes to get there, and I coach managers on communication that moves people to act.

Do you also work with freelancers and individuals? Yes, but rarely. You're welcome to leave your details – I try to find time for a short conversation even when I don't have room to take on a full engagement.

In my day job I'm a product manager at a large international company – running complex systems and global teams. I bring those tools, together with a sharp eye for people and process, to my work with managers and organizations across every sector.

I'll admit it: more than 20 years of a career that's wandered in all kinds of directions.

Galei Tzahal (Army Radio). Law and psychology. Organizational consulting studies at the Hebrew University. Work across all three sectors, in Israel and the United States, in product, operations, business development, and management.

Today I connect all of those dots through the Weekly Sync podcast and in my work with organizations and managers.
